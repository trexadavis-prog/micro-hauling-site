What we take, and what we don't

We are two people and a pickup with a trailer. That keeps prices low. Most jobs are one trip; bigger piles are priced by the trip, so you know the whole number before we start.

We take

  • Furniture, mattresses, appliances, and electronics
  • Garage, basement, and shed clean-outs
  • Move-out and rental turnover clean-outs
  • Yard debris: branches, leaves, old fence boards
  • Bagged trash and boxed items
  • Trampolines, swing sets, and bed frames (we take them apart)
  • Small business and landlord jobs
  • Light demolition, quoted case by case

We don't take

  • Paint, chemicals, oil, propane, or batteries
  • Concrete, dirt, brick, tile, or shingles
  • Pianos, hot tubs, safes, pool tables, cast-iron tubs
  • Hoarding or biohazard clean-ups
  • More than two tires
  • Loose food waste or unbagged debris

We reserve the right to refuse any item on arrival.

Usable furniture and goods go to Deseret Industries or Habitat ReStore when we can. Metal gets recycled. The dump is our last stop, not our first.
