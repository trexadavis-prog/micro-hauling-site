What we take, and what we don't

We are two people and a pickup with a trailer. That keeps prices low. Most jobs are one trip; bigger piles are priced by the trip, so you know the whole number before we start.

We take

  • Furniture, mattresses, appliances, and electronics
  • Garage, basement, and shed clean-outs
  • Move-out and rental turnover clean-outs
  • Yard debris: branches, leaves, old fence boards
  • Bagged trash and boxed items
  • Trampolines, swing sets, and bed frames (we take them apart)
  • Small business and landlord jobs
  • Light demolition, quoted case by case

We don't take

  • Paint, chemicals, oil, propane, or batteries
  • Concrete, dirt, brick, tile, or shingles
  • Pianos, hot tubs, safes, pool tables, cast-iron tubs
  • Hoarding or biohazard clean-ups
  • More than two tires
  • Loose food waste or unbagged debris

We reserve the right to refuse any item on arrival.

Usable furniture and goods go to Deseret Industries or Habitat ReStore when we can. Metal gets recycled. The dump is our last stop, not our first.

How much does junk removal cost in Provo?

Three prices, the same every day: $75 for one large item, $150 for a half load (the truck bed full), and $250 for a full load (truck and trailer both full). Every price includes labor, carry-out, loading, dump fees, and a sweep. Cash or Venmo.

A few items cost the dump extra, so they cost a little extra here too, and we tell you the whole number before we come.

Do you take mattresses, fridges, and TVs?

All three. The dump charges us extra for them, so we pass that along: a mattress or box spring is +$25, a fridge, freezer, or AC unit is +$20, and a TV or other electronics is +$15. Tires are +$10 each, two per job.

What won't you take?

Paint, chemicals, oil, propane, and batteries. Concrete, dirt, brick, tile, and shingles. Pianos, hot tubs, safes, pool tables, and cast-iron tubs. Hoarding and biohazard clean-ups. More than two tires, and loose or unbagged food waste.

How soon can you come?

Text a photo to 385-204-6385 and you will usually have a firm price the same day. We haul Saturdays 7am to 6pm and weekdays 5pm to 9pm. Weeknights are truck-bed jobs; anything that needs the trailer is a Saturday.

Do I have to carry it out to the driveway myself?

No. Carrying it out is included in the price, upstairs and basements too. You point, we lift. If you would rather stack it in the driveway before we arrive, that takes $10 off.

Is the price you text me the price I pay?

Yes. No hourly rate, no surprise at the end. If the pile turns out bigger than the photos showed, we tell you the new price before we touch anything, and you are free to say no.

What towns do you serve?

Provo and everywhere within 25 miles, which covers nearly all of Utah County: Orem, Vineyard, Lindon, Pleasant Grove, American Fork, Lehi, Saratoga Springs, Springville, Mapleton, Spanish Fork, Salem, and Payson. Farther than 25 miles adds a flat $20.

What happens to my stuff after you take it?

Usable furniture and goods go to Deseret Industries or Habitat ReStore when we can, and metal gets recycled. The dump is our last stop, not our first.
