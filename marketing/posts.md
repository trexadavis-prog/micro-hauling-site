# Posts to copy and paste

Ready-made listings and posts for Facebook Marketplace and Nextdoor. Everything below matches
the prices and hours on the website as of 2026-09-09. If you change a price on the site, change
it here too before you post again.

**Before you post anything: you need one photo.** Facebook Marketplace will not let you publish a
listing without one, and a Nextdoor post without a photo gets scrolled past. The truck with the
trailer hitched, both of you at the tailgate, truck wiped down, taken in daylight. One photo
unblocks every post on this page.

**Two things never to write:** do not say "insured" or "licensed." Neither is true yet, and on
Nextdoor a neighbor will ask. Everything below is written to be honest without drawing attention
to it.

# Facebook Marketplace

Marketplace is a search engine, not a feed. Nobody browses for "a hauling company" — they search
for the exact thing in their garage. So post **five separate listings**, one per search, instead
of one listing that mentions everything. They all point to the same phone number and cost nothing
extra.

**Mechanics:** Category is *Services*. Listings go stale after about a week, so open each one and
hit **Renew** every Sunday night. Marketplace replies land in Messenger, so turn notifications on —
the first person to reply usually gets the job.

# Nextdoor

Nextdoor is the opposite of Marketplace. It's a feed of neighbors, and it punishes anything that
reads like an ad — people flag it, and the algorithm buries it. What works is sounding like a
person who lives there, because you do.

**Mechanics:** post from a **Business Page**, not your personal profile, or it gets removed. Post
roughly **once every two or three weeks** — more than that and neighbors start muting you. The
single most valuable thing on Nextdoor is a **Recommendation**: after every job, ask the customer
to recommend you on Nextdoor. Ten of those outperform anything you can write yourself.

# Also worth doing

**Facebook local buy/sell groups** are separate from Marketplace and often better. Search for and
join groups like "Utah County Buy Sell Trade," "Provo/Orem Classifieds," and any ward or
neighborhood group you're already in. Post the Listing 1 text there once a week, rotating groups.
Read each group's rules first — some have a fixed day for service posts.

**Ask for the review every single time.** At the end of every job, while you're standing there:
"If you were happy with this, a Google review helps us a lot more than anything else we can do."
The first ten reviews are worth more than every post on this page combined.

# The graphics (marketing/graphics/)

Five 1200×1200 images, built from the site's own colors, wordmark and mountain ridge. Square works
on both Marketplace and Nextdoor. Every one carries the phone number and the web address, so it
still sells if someone screenshots it and sends it to a friend.

**These are graphics, not photos, and that is the limit of what they do.** They unblock posting
today, and a price list is genuinely the right first image for a Marketplace listing. But the
photo of the two of you at the tailgate still outperforms all five, because people are deciding
whether to let strangers into their house. Use these until you have that photo, then use both.
